Overview

Benefits

Accessible Experience

KYOMUN is a highly accessible MUN conference that is free of cost and held online.

Beginner-Oriented Experience

KYOMUN will host an educational session teaching basic MUN procedures and tips. Individual feedback will also be provided for delegates.

High Quality Experience

Secretariats and Chairs are highly experienced MUN delegates that have undergone rigorous training to provide the best conference experience.
